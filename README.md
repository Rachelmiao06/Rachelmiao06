## 🧪 Small experimental projects

🧶 [Tada](https://github.com/Rachelmiao06/bostonhacks-2026) · [Live demo](https://bostonhacks-2026.vercel.app/)<br>
A civic feedback loop on a pixel-art map. Tada brings MBTA alerts, Boston 311 cases, and rider observations together so riders can see what's broken, snap a photo to draft a 311 report with AI, and follow the issue until the city closes it. Every contribution adds a knitted piece to your own yarn island, so the world you build reflects how you've helped the city. Built at BostonHacks 2026.

🗳️ [Legible](https://github.com/Rachelmiao06/legible) · [Live demo](https://nine-five-ochre.vercel.app/)<br>
A social-style feed for Massachusetts ballot questions, bills, meetings, and deadlines. Legible uses a profile stored in your browser to rank your For You feed and tailor plain-language explanations. Explore lets you browse more broadly, and every quote is checked word for word against official sources. Built spec-first with Kiro for the civic track at Hackachusetts 2026.

❄️ [Plop!](https://github.com/Rachelmiao06/plop) · [Play it](https://plop-two.vercel.app/)<br>
A Christmas game for friends and an experiment in mixing materials. Roll a snowball through soft, realistic snow and watch a crowd of flat, hand-drawn paper characters topple over. Then send a challenge to your group chat and turn it into a tiny snowball league.

🍳 [Egg Breakfast Comic](https://github.com/Rachelmiao06/egg-breakfast-comic) · [Read it](https://egg-breakfast-comic.vercel.app/preview/visual)<br>
A Chinese-language web comic about my mom cooking breakfast for her "宝宝", told from the point of view of a fried egg that turns out to be me. Scrolling choreographs the sound and motion: scroll-triggered audio carries the story, layered parallax makes still drawings feel animated, and the egg leaps out of its panel and onto Mom's spatula.
